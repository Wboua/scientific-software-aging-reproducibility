# Research Questions and Case Mapping

## RQ1 — Contemporary executability
**To what extent can published scientific software artifacts still be installed, built, and executed in contemporary environments?**
Primary cases: all, with H-aware eligibility.

## RQ2 — Failure mechanisms
**What technical mechanisms prevent or complicate long-term reproducibility?**
Strong cases: PS002, PS004, PS005, PS006, PS007, PS008, PS009, PS010.

## RQ3 — Repairability
**To what extent can initially failing artifacts be recovered through minimal interventions, and what repairs are required?**
Strong cases: PS004, PS005, PS008, PS009, PS010.

## RQ4 — Numerical/scientific fidelity
**When execution is recovered, are the original scientific/benchmark correctness criteria preserved?**
Strong cases: PS001 (when trace recovered/rerun), PS005, PS008, PS009, PS010.

## RQ5 — Performance fidelity
**Does successful numerical reproduction imply preservation of computational performance?**
Strong cases: PS003 and PS008; PS009 can be contextual/supporting.

## RQ6 — Preservation mechanisms
**To what extent do containers, virtual machines, dependency specifications, and build systems protect artifacts from software and hardware aging?**
Strong cases: PS002 Docker+GPU, PS005 capsule/environment, PS007 VM, PS008/PS009 build metadata, PS010 CRAN ecosystem.
